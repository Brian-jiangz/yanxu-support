Common questions

How do I back up or move my records?

Open Profile → Settings → Backup and export a JSON file. Restoring a backup replaces the records it covers. Save a copy of your current data first.

Why am I missing reminders?

Allow notifications for Yanxu in iOS settings and check the reminder time on the relevant task, event, or habit.

How do I stop Zotero imports?

Clear the Zotero user ID and API key in Yanxu Settings. You can also revoke the key in your Zotero account.

Do I need an account?

No. Research records and schedules stay on your device. DOI lookup, Zotero imports, and external digests are optional online features.

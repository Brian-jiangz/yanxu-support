Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Data on your device

Yanxu works without an account. Your profile, research logs, literature notes, projects, tasks, schedule, habits, ledger, and profile photo are stored on your device. The developer has no server that receives these records. Yanxu uses no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. The app and its widgets share some data on your device to show your events, tasks, progress, and Live Activities. Yanxu can add tasks, papers, and logs to system search on your device.

Optional online features

When you look up a paper by DOI, Yanxu sends the DOI to Crossref to retrieve publication metadata.

When you preview or refresh literature tracking, Yanxu sends your search keywords and date range to Crossref. Once you save the search, Yanxu refreshes it periodically when you open the app. The topic label, tracking settings, and cached results are kept in local app settings, outside the exported Yanxu JSON backup. You can stop automatic searches by unfollowing the topic or deleting its project. Papers you saved to your library remain available.

If you set up Zotero imports, Yanxu sends your Zotero user ID and API key to the Zotero API and requests metadata and attachments from your selected library. The credentials are stored in the device keychain. You can remove them in Yanxu settings to stop future access or revoke the key in your Zotero account.

If you add a raw GitHub Gist URL for the literature digest, Yanxu contacts that URL to download the digest. Clear the URL in Settings to stop future access. Opening an external link for a paper connects to the website that hosts it.

External services process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. They may log connection details such as your IP address. Yanxu connects to those services when you use the relevant features.

Notifications and photos

With your permission, Yanxu sends local reminders for tasks, events, habits, and the morning briefing. You can turn off notifications in device settings. Yanxu uses the system photo picker to access the photo you select for your profile.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Yanxu keeps about seven days of automatic local backups. A JSON export from Settings includes your profile and supported research and personal records. You choose where to share or save the export.

You can delete records from their respective screens. To remove Yanxu data from your device, clear your Zotero credentials and uninstall Yanxu and its widgets. Manage any files you exported or shared in their saved locations, and manage data held by external services with the respective provider. Device backups are managed in your system settings.
